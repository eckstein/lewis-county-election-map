About this site Unofficial

VoteMap WA shows Washington election data precinct by precinct, starting with Lewis County.

What you can see

  • Lewis County map: how many ballots each precinct has returned, and who leads each contest in each precinct, shaded by the size of the lead. You can compare with past elections, look up your precinct by address, and open any contest as a table or download it as a CSV.
  • National races: live results for 2026 U.S. Senate, U.S. House and governor races, including Lewis County's congressional district.

More Washington counties are coming for the 2027 elections.

Where the data comes from

Results and ballots countedLewis County election results, with statewide and district totals from the Washington Secretary of State
Ballots receivedThe Secretary of State's daily ballot status reports, totaled by precinct
Precinct boundariesThe Secretary of State's precinct shapefiles
National racesThe People's Tally (open data, CC BY 4.0), with race ratings from Cook Political Report

Results load live from these sources while the page is open. On election night the map checks every minute; the rest of the time it checks less often, since the county and state each post about once a day. The state posts ballot returns each business day around 5 p.m.

Official results

This site isn't affiliated with Lewis County, the Washington Secretary of State, or any news organization. Its numbers are unofficial until they're certified. For official results, see the Lewis County Auditor and the Secretary of State.

Privacy

  • Addresses you search for are looked up by the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder and aren't stored or logged.
  • "Use my location" stays on your device.
  • Ballot return files list individual voters, but this site only publishes precinct totals.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track individuals.
