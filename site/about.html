About VoteMapWA

An independent, unofficial map of Washington election data, precinct by precinct. It starts with Lewis County.

This site is not affiliated with Lewis County, the Washington Secretary of State, or any campaign or party. Results here are unofficial until certified by the Lewis County Auditor and the Secretary of State.

What's here

  • Lewis County map: ballots received and election results for every precinct in the county, with comparisons to past elections and to statewide results.
  • National races: live results for U.S. Senate, U.S. House and governor races across the country, with Lewis County's own congressional race (WA-03) at the top.

Only Lewis County is mapped for now. More Washington counties are coming for the 2027 elections.

Where the data comes from

  • Results come live from the county's and the state's official results feeds at results.votewa.gov. On election night the map checks for new numbers every minute.
  • Ballots received come from the Secretary of State's daily ballot status report, usually posted around 5 p.m. That report lists individual voters, so the site only ever publishes precinct totals.
  • Precinct boundaries are the Secretary of State's precinct shapefiles.
  • National races come from The People's Tally (CC BY 4.0), which gathers counts from state election offices. Race calls are shown only when a named source such as the AP makes them, and ratings are Cook Political Report's.
  • Past elections such as the 2022 General use certified results saved from the Secretary of State and the MIT Election Data and Science Lab.

Privacy

There are no accounts, ads or tracking cookies. Addresses you search for are looked up by the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder and aren't stored, and "Use my location" never leaves your device. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track individuals.

Corrections and source code

The site is open source. If something looks wrong, or you have an idea, open an issue on GitHub. For official numbers, always check the county and state sites linked above.
