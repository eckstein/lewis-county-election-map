Lewis County and Washington results come live from the county's and the Secretary of State's official results feeds at results.votewa.gov. Some national counts come from Open America's feed of official state counts, some leader percentages come from watch.vote's results feed, Pushbuild's API and Pollgebra (which gathers network results pages) fill in states without a free feed, and a few are entered by hand from state and AP results pages where no feed is available. National results and race ratings come from The People's Tally (open data, CC BY 4.0), which gathers unofficial counts from state election offices and race calls from named sources. Ratings are Cook Political Report's. Numbers are unofficial until each state certifies them; follow each race's source link for the official count.
